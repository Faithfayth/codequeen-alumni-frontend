About Educating The Children & CodeQueen

Our Core Mission

Founded in 2009, Educating The Children (ETC) has dedicated itself to transforming lives across East Africa by bridging generational gaps in education, resource access, and systemic equity. Over its operating history, ETC has supported more than 6,000 young women through direct structural initiatives.

In 2019, ETC launched its cornerstone technology vehicle in Kampala, Uganda—the Code Queen program. Designed as an intensive, practical coding academy, Code Queen equips young women from disadvantaged backgrounds with comprehensive technical skills, including Full-Stack Web Development, UI/UX Design, Entrepreneurship, and cutting-edge **Artificial Intelligence engineering**, transforming them into high-value developers and community leaders.

Evolution & Milestones
2009 — Foundation of Educating The Children (ETC)

Established to support vulnerable youth through infrastructural frameworks, including building schools and providing educational scholarships in rural sectors.

2019 — Code Queen Flagship Launch in Kampala

Pivoted toward direct digital solutions, introducing an intensive software boot-camp structure to bridge the technical employment divide for women.

2021 — Scaled Digital & Hybrid Infrastructures

Introduced scalable online delivery mechanisms following changing workspace climates, extending learning avenues past traditional boundaries.

2025-2026 — The AI Frontier & Sisterhood Ecosystem Upgrade

Integrated advanced modules covering AI prompt engineering, task automation, and professional system design, giving rise to an alumni-led network model.
